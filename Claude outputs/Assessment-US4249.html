Understanding Workplace Expectations

Employee Obligations Assessment

US4249 (Version 10), Pathways Education

Describe obligations as an employee

Level 1 Internal Assessment
3 Credits

What to do

You will complete four tasks. You must base your answers on a workplace that is NOT your school or an educational placement.

You do not need to currently have a job. You can choose:

  • A workplace you have worked in before
  • A workplace where a family member works
  • A workplace you are interested in working in (e.g., retail, construction, hospitality)

Your answers should reflect realistic workplace expectations, policies, and practices.

Task 1 – Personal Presentation

  1. Describe your workplace context.
    This must be a workplace you are familiar with or can access reliable information about (for example, a previous job, a whānau or community workplace, or a place where you understand how staff work).

    In your description, include:

    1. The type of workplace
    2. The work that employees do
  2. Describe the personal presentation requirements for this workplace (including safety).

    Include at least five requirements, such as clothing, footwear, hair, jewellery, personal hygiene, personal protective equipment (PPE).

    For each requirement:

    1. Describe what is expected
    2. Explain why it is important in this workplace

Task 4 – Time and Organisation

Describe how an employee shows responsibility in the workplace:

  1. Time management and punctuality

    I would manage my time by…

    I would make sure I arrive on time by…

  2. Use of devices

    What should you do with your device?

    In this workplace, my device should…

    When can you use your device?

    I would only use my device when…

  3. Breaks

    I would take my breaks at…

    My breaks would be… minutes long

    This is important because…

  4. Personal appointments

    I would try to make personal appointments by…

    If I had to attend an appointment during work time, I would…

  5. Absences

    If I was going to be absent, I would inform…

    I would do this (when/how)…

    This is important because…

  6. Deadlines

    If I could not meet a task or production deadline, I would…

    This is important because…

  7. Care of equipment
    • I would take care of equipment by…
    • If something was broken, I would…

How to present your learning

Complete written responses (typed or neatly handwritten)

Use:

  • Full sentences or bullet points
  • Workplace-specific examples

Make sure your answers:

  • Are clear and detailed
  • Relate to your chosen workplace

Conditions:

This is an individual assessment activity and all work must be your own. You may use your PWY1001 learning materials and class notes to support your understanding, but you must complete the assessment without direct assistance. Plagiarism detection may be used to ensure that your work is original.

Timeframe

Work at your own pace, but ensure all tasks are completed before submission.

Getting started

Begin by reading through the full assessment so you understand what is required for each task. Take time to think about how your chosen workplace operates, including its rules, expectations, health and safety practices, and typical staff responsibilities. Use your PWY1001 learning materials and class notes to support your understanding, and plan your ideas before you start writing. It can be helpful to brainstorm examples for each task and use key terms such as expectations, responsibilities, safety, and professional behaviour to guide your responses.

Student Resources

You can use a range of resources to support your learning as you complete this assessment. This includes your PWY1001 Trust and Care module, class notes, and any kaiako-provided materials about workplace expectations and employment responsibilities. You may also draw on examples of workplace policies such as uniform requirements, behaviour expectations, and health and safety procedures to help you develop realistic answers.

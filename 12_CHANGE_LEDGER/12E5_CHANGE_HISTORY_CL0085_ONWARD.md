| CL-0085 | Wednesday, 26th August, 2026 | **NEW constraint 85 — a THREE-part `[TITLE BAR]` builds THREE `<h1><span>` titles, in the Languages cohort ONLY.** Languages writers give the module title in three languages, `|`-separated — `[TITLE BAR] HALLO, DEUTSCH! | HELLO, GERMAN! | NAU MAI KI TE REO TIAMANA!` (`GENO901`) — but the KB's only title rule was the two-title English / Te Reo split, so the conversion merged the first two parts into one heading (`Begrüßung und Vorstellung | Welcome and Introduction`). **THE RULE:** for a Languages-cohort module (Chinese, French, Gagana Samoan, German, Japanese, Spanish) each `|`-separated part of a three-part title bar becomes its **own** `<h1><span>` on the overview page, **in the writer's order** — the cohort uses both orders (`GENO901`, `GEFUN01`, `SPA1004` and the Samoan `1005 Sasa` lead with the target language; `GEFUN06` leads with English), so the conversion never re-orders. An empty part, or an unfilled placeholder (`TE REO`, `MODULE TITLE TE REO`, `INSERT MODULE TITLE TE REO`), is **skipped**; fewer than two real titles after skipping → `Red Flag:`. **SCOPED DELIBERATELY — the universal form was measured and REJECTED.** The change was requested universally (split on every `|`). Pre-change measurement over the whole connected corpus — **3,981 `.docx` scanned, all 1,472 `[TITLE BAR]` lines** (941 no pipe, 513 one pipe, **11 modules** with two pipes) — found only **5** of the 11 genuinely trilingual, **every one Languages-cohort**; the human-built HTML for the other **6** ships **two** headings: `ANZH304` keeps the pipe *inside* the heading (`<h1><span>Mana Motuhake | Te Tiriti o Waitangi</span></h1>`), `XGF9003` joins an English title + English subtitle with an en-dash, `HIS1007` / `HIS1008` drop a second title pair, and `TEFUN01` / `TEFUN04` drop the unfilled `TE REO` placeholder. A `|` legitimately survives inside a finished `<h1>` in three modules (`ANZH304`; `HIS1001` — `Independence | Colonisation | Independence`; `XMES201` — `My Pets | Aku Mokai`, six pages), and **no** page in the 2,385-page finalized corpus carries a genuine three-language title (all 37 three-`<h1>` pages are artefacts — `Lorem Ipsum` template residue, `Maori title not provided` notes, or duplicates). The universal form would have regressed 6 modules to gain 5. Outside the cohort the two-title rule is unchanged and a two-pipe title bar emits the pair plus a visible `Red Flag:` quoting the original. **Order left as the writer's** — the finalized corpus does re-order the *two*-title case to English-first (`TEFUN03`), but there is no built precedent for the three-title case, so no re-ordering is invented; raised with the designer and deferred. | (b) Series-scoped — Languages cohort (Chinese, French, Gagana Samoan, German, Japanese, Spanish) | `01A` → Template Title Patterns → **TITLE BAR PARSING RULE** (new “THREE-PART `[TITLE BAR]` — LANGUAGES COHORT ONLY” block + the non-Languages guard); `14A` → § 14.1 Languages Phase 1–4 (new three-part title bullet); `00G` → **NEW constraint 85**; `02C` → Headings & Titles checklist (two new lines); `02D` → policy item 17 `always_split_dual_titles` | Direct-typed (Update Mode) — designer request, 26 August 2026 (Chris); scoped after pre-change corpus measurement | Major | Implemented | Designer decision (Chris) — approved the scoped Languages-only form after the measurement showed the universal form would break 6 modules. **Part 1 (Locked) is EMPTY — nothing blockable.** No prior CL row covers three-part title bars; `CL-0069` / `CL-0076` (constraint 79, lesson-page titles) are untouched. |
